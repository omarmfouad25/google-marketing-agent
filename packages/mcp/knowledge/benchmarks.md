# Reading performance

How to judge CPC, CTR and cost per lead without trusting generic industry averages.

- **Use the account's own baseline**: compare against the same campaign type over the previous 4–8 weeks, not against published averages.
- **Search**: judge on cost per lead and search-term relevance. A high CTR with irrelevant search terms is waste; low impression share on a profitable campaign is the opportunity.
- **Demand Gen / display-style**: cheap clicks are normal; judge on engaged sessions and cost per lead, not CPC.
- **Lead quality beats lead volume**: check leads against CRM outcomes before scaling a campaign with a low CPL.
- **Minimum data before acting**: wait for roughly two weeks or about 30 conversions per campaign before changing bidding strategy; budget and negative changes can happen sooner.
- **One change at a time per campaign**, logged, so the effect is readable (the change log + `changes_list` make this easy).

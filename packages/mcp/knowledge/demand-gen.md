# Demand Gen

Building and reading Demand Gen campaigns.

- **Structure**: one goal per campaign (custom goal), budget at campaign level, locations + languages per ad group, presence-only.
- **Images**: landscape 1.91:1, square 1:1, portrait 4:5, logo 1:1. Several per ratio. No painted buttons or fake UI.
- **Text**: up to 5 headlines (≤40 chars), 5 descriptions (≤90), business name (≤25), optional CTA text.
- **Audience is the main lever.** Without one, optimized targeting starts from scratch and B2B ads reach consumers. Build: CRM Customer Match list + its lookalike + a custom segment of buyer-intent search terms and competitor/vendor URLs. Keep website-visitor lists out of B2B audiences when consumer campaigns drive site traffic.
- **Channels**: prefer Google-owned channels (YouTube, Discover, Gmail) at launch. Check the placement split (`segments.ad_network_type`) after a few days; image ads often end up mostly on YouTube with weak click quality.
- **Reading week one**: learning status, CPM/CTR by channel, engaged sessions in GA4 vs clicks, cost per lead against GA4 leads. Expect uneven delivery for the first days; adding or changing an audience can restart learning.
